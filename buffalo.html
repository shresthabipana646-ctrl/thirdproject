About Buffalo

Buffalo is a domestic animal commonly raised for milk, meat and agricultural purposes. It is an important animal in Nepal and many other countries.

Types of Buffalo

  • Murrah Buffalo
  • Jaffarabadi Buffalo
  • Surti Buffalo
  • Local Buffalo

Importance of Buffalo

Buffaloes provide several benefits:

  1. Provides nutritious milk.
  2. Provides meat.
  3. Helps in farming activities.
  4. Provides manure for agriculture.
  5. Supports farmers' income.

Food of Buffalo

Buffaloes mainly eat grass, green fodder, hay and other agricultural feed. They also need clean drinking water.

Buffalo Products

Products Uses
Milk Making curd, cheese and butter
Meat Food
Manure Organic fertilizer
